# Midterms

Each midterm is named for the year it was given. Past years are kept so you have
practice midterms; the current year is the one you take.

| Year | Instructions | Example script | Part 2 data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall 2026 | `Midterm_2026.md` (and `.docx`) | `midterm_2026_example.R` | `midterm_2026_2a.RDS`, `_2b`, `_2c` |
| Fall 2025 | `Midterm_2025.md` (and `.docx`) | `midterm_2025_example.R` | `midterm_2025_2a.RDS`, `_2b`, `_2c` |

For the instructor: `build_midterm_2026_data.R` builds the 2026 data, and
`render_midterm.R` renders every `Midterm_*.md` to Word and HTML.
